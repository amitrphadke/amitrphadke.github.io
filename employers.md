## What each stint was for

**UST — Software Architect, DevSecOps.** Designing and governing secure-by-default CI/CD with security and quality gates at every stage; cloud infrastructure as code with Terraform and Helm on Kubernetes; SAST, policy-as-code and container scanning embedded in delivery; observability and platform troubleshooting; guiding engineering teams on architecture standards.

**Infosys — Principal Consultant, DevOps SRE.** CI/CD pipeline design and maintenance, infrastructure automation, monitoring and troubleshooting, containerisation and orchestration, and integrating security practice into DevOps pipelines. Cisco dCloud and Adidas e-commerce were the two large engagements.

**Sonora Software — Cloud Solution Architect.** Designing scalable, secure and cost-effective cloud architectures, and providing guidance on hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

**Infosys — Technology Lead.** Leading design and implementation of complex technical solutions, mentoring development teams, owning end-to-end delivery, evaluating emerging technology, and setting coding and quality standards.

**TCS, IT People, J &amp; J Consultancy.** The Microsoft-stack years — C#, ASP.NET, SharePoint, Oracle — including migration of mainframe online screens to web applications.
