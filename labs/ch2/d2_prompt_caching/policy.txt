ACME SUPPORT POLICY HANDBOOK (internal)
1. Refunds. Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any reason. Between 31 and 60 days a refund is possible only for defective products and requires a support ticket with photos. After 60 days no refunds are issued; store credit may be offered at the manager's discretion. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Subscription refunds are pro-rated to the day of cancellation.
2. Discounts. Front-line agents may apply discounts up to 10% on a single order. Discounts between 10% and 20% require a team lead. Any discount over 20% must be approved by the regional sales manager in writing. Discounts never stack with promotional codes. Educational and non-profit customers receive a standing 15% discount after verification.
3. Shipping. Standard shipping is free over 2,000 INR. Express shipping is charged at cost. International orders may attract customs duties which the customer pays. Lost parcels are re-shipped once tracking shows no movement for 10 business days. Damaged deliveries are replaced when reported within 48 hours with photos.
4. Warranty. Hardware carries a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, water ingress and unauthorised repairs void the warranty. Warranty claims are handled by the returns team; the customer receives a prepaid label. Replacement units are refurbished unless the original was less than 30 days old.
5. Data and privacy. Agents must never ask for full card numbers, passwords or one-time codes. Verify identity with the order number plus the email or phone on file. Personal data is retained for 24 months after the last order. Customers can request export or deletion; deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Never paste customer data into third-party tools.
6. Escalation. Threats, legal mentions, or media enquiries are escalated to the duty manager immediately. Repeated contacts (three or more on one issue) are escalated to tier 2. Outages affecting more than 50 customers trigger the incident process and a status page update within 15 minutes.
7. Tone. Be brief, warm and specific. Apologise once, then fix. Never promise dates the fulfilment system does not show. Close every ticket with a summary of what was done and what the customer should expect next.
